## Accessibility

Behaviour, focus management, and ARIA wiring come from [Base UI's Toggle Group](https://base-ui.com/react/components/toggle-group) — see its reference for the full keyboard map.

- Disabled parts mute their contents rather than the element, keeping the focus ring at full strength where the part stays focusable.
